General Questions

1. What is Student Hub?

Student Hub is an online portal where students can access academic information, events, profiles, and other services.

2. How do I register?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, and submit the registration form.

3. I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact the administrator or use the password recovery option if available.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes. After logging in, visit the Profile page to edit your personal information.

5. How can I participate in events?

Visit the Events page, read the event details, and complete the registration process.
